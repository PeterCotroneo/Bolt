about=Bolt streams live lightning strikes into QGIS and shows them flash onto the map as they happen, then fade over a minute — track your current map view or draw an area to watch. Strikes come free and keyless from the Blitzortung.org volunteer lightning-detection network (data © Blitzortung.org contributors, for non-commercial use). Strikes are coloured by age (a bright flash that fades) and busy storms cluster into counted badges. Data is live only — Bolt does not replay history. It is the storm-side companion to Wake (marine vessels), Contrail (aircraft) and Zenith (satellites), built on the same live-tracking engine.
